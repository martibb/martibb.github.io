Lunar rover perception

Done for my MSc thesis at the University of Pisa, as a visiting researcher at the European Astronaut Centre (ESA) in Cologne, within the Spaceship Team.

Domain-Aware Curriculum Learning for Lunar Rover Rock Detection Under Realistic Illumination Conditions

Poster presented at [Conference name, year]

A YOLO-based rock detector for the rover Odin, trained with a curriculum that moves from generic data to indoor lighting and then to polar-like sunlight. Adding depth to the RGB image through a mid-fusion network keeps detection reliable where appearance alone fails. Tested at the LUNA Analog Facility in Cologne.
